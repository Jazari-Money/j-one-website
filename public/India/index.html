FAQ

How do I get the extra $10?

Download Jazari One, verify your identity, add money from your US bank and send $200 or more to India in one transfer. We add $10 on top, so your family gets it together with your money.

Who can get it?

People who live in the US (outside New York), have an SSN or ITIN and make their first international transfer with Jazari One. It counts whether you found us through an ad or on your own.

When is the $10 added?

Right in your first transfer of $200 or more. Your family receives it together with the money you send.

What does a transfer cost?

There is no separate transfer fee: our exchange rate already includes every fee. The app shows exactly how much arrives before you confirm.

How do I add money?

From your US bank by ACH (usually 1 to 3 business days), FedNow or wire.

Which Indian accounts can receive?

Savings and NRO accounts. NRE accounts are coming soon.

Why did my family get ₹1 first?

The first time you send to someone new, their bank gets ₹1 to confirm the account, then the full amount right after.
