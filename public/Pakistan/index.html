Questions

How do I get the $10 bonus?

Download Jazari One, verify your identity, add money from your US bank and send $200 or more to Pakistan in one transfer. 30 days later, $10 lands in your Jazari One balance.

Who can get it?

People who live in the US (outside New York), have an SSN or ITIN and make their first international transfer with Jazari One. It counts whether you found us through an ad or on your own.

When is the bonus paid?

30 days after your transfer, once it has completed and has not been reversed.

What does a transfer cost?

There is no separate transfer fee: our exchange rate already includes every fee. The app shows exactly how much arrives before you confirm.

How do I add money?

From your US bank by ACH (usually 1 to 3 business days), FedNow or wire.

Where can my family receive?

In their bank account or mobile wallet in Pakistan.
